Physiotherapy in Bitez, Bodrum

A private physiotherapy unit where three physiotherapists work across manual therapy, scoliosis-specific exercise, clinical and reformer Pilates, and pelvic floor rehabilitation.

Assessment and treatment are planned around your needs, your current function and your doctor's referral. Please bring your doctor's report and any X-ray or MRI results to the first visit.

Treatments

  • Lower backOsteopathic manual therapyHands-on techniques for pain and restricted movement in the spine and joints, always combined with an exercise programme.
  • Upper backScoliosis · 3D SchrothScoliosis-specific exercise built on corrective positions, three-dimensional elongation and corrective breathing, alongside your orthopaedic doctor's care.
  • NeckClinical PilatesStarts with a posture analysis; stabilisation, mobility and resistance exercises supervised by a physiotherapist.
  • Lower backReformer PilatesSpring resistance set to your level, in one-to-one or at most two-person sessions.
  • PelvisPelvic floor and visceral osteopathyPelvic floor rehabilitation and gentle manual techniques for the abdomen and pelvis. Diagnosis of complaints in this area is made by a doctor.

Treatment pages and health articles are currently in Turkish.

How it works

Assessment

At the first visit we go through your doctor's report, imaging and history, and assess range of motion, muscle strength, posture and the limits you notice in daily life.

Programme

A treatment and exercise programme is built for you. We explain its aim, how long it lasts and how often sessions take place.

Follow-up

Your response is monitored at every session, the programme is updated and exercises progress step by step.

Please bring: your doctor's report, any X-ray or MRI results, and a list of medicines you take regularly.
